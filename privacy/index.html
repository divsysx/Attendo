Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026 · Applies to the Attendo Android app and this website.

The short version

Attendo is local-first. Your attendance data, timetable, and settings live on your phone. Two things can leave it, and only if you choose them: anonymous room reports you post to the community board, and — if you sign in with GitHub — the private copy of your attendance that Attendo keeps for your account so your other phones can stay in step. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't build advertising profiles.

What stays on your device

  • Your courses, timetable, attendance records, notes, and settings — stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Backups and exports — complete app backups are saved in JSON format, while attendance data can be exported in CSV format. Files are created only when you choose to export and are not uploaded by the app.

If you never sign in and never post a report, nothing you record leaves your phone.

Optional community reporting

Attendo can send anonymous room-availability reports to a shared community board so other students can see which rooms are free right now. If you use this feature, your report content (room, time, observation type) and a randomly generated community identifier are sent to our backend and shown to other users. Community features can be used without an account.

That identifier is not your name, your email address, or a device identifier, and it is not linked to your attendance records. Reports you post are visible to other Attendo users, so please don't put anything personal in them.

GitHub sign-in

Attendo offers optional sign-in with GitHub. Signing in happens on GitHub's own page: Attendo never sees your GitHub password, and it receives no access to your repositories.

Attendo uses the result for one thing — recognising which account is yours. It reads your account identifier and whether that account has a GitHub login attached to it, and nothing else. It does not read your name, email address, profile picture, or any other profile information. The name Attendo greets you with is a display name you type yourself in Settings.

You can also use Attendo without signing in at all, in which case none of this applies. You can disconnect at any time (see below).

Syncing your attendance

Signing in with GitHub also turns on attendance syncing. This is what makes a second phone, or the same phone after a reinstall, carry on where you left off — and it is the only way your attendance is ever uploaded.

What is uploaded, to a private copy held for your account and readable by no other user:

  • Your courses — names, codes, whether each is archived, and each course's own attendance target.
  • Your recurring timetable patterns — day, start hour, length, room, faculty code, and class kind.
  • Your individual class records — date, hour, whether you attended and why not, room, class kind, and any note you attached to that class.
  • Your semesters, including archived ones, and your attendance history within them.
  • Your settings — term dates, holidays, working Saturdays, your overall target, the default target for new courses, your section and batch, your display name, and whether you joined mid-semester and when.

How it is kept in step. Each row carries the time your phone last changed it, and the most recent change wins. Deletions are recorded rather than erased immediately, so that deleting a course on one phone also removes it from your other phones instead of being restored by them. Sync runs on its own while you are online, and Sync now in Settings forces it immediately.

What it is not. It is not shared with other students, it is not used for anything except syncing your own devices, and it is not read by us except where you ask us to help with a problem or ask us to delete it. Signing out stops syncing but deletes nothing.

Analytics

The app collects anonymous, aggregate usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics. What is recorded is a small set of counters about the in-app update flow — that an update check was made, that an update was found, that a download was started, completed or failed verification, and that an install was launched. These events carry no parameters, no screen names, and nothing about your courses or attendance, and they are not linked to your name, your email, your account, or the content of your records.

Attendo shows no ads and uses no advertising identifier; the advertising-ID permission the analytics library asks for is explicitly removed from the app.

What we never do

  • Sell your personal data.
  • Share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Upload your attendance, backups, or exports — except to the private copy held for your own account, when you have signed in and syncing is on.
  • Read your GitHub profile beyond which account you are.
  • Send you marketing or sell advertising.

Data retention and deletion

  • Device data: delete it any time by clearing Attendo's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Community data: signing out of the community feature removes the community identity from your device; reports you have contributed remain on the community board unless you ask us to remove them.
  • Account data: the copy held for your account stays there until it is removed, so that your phones can keep syncing. To delete your account and everything held for it — the attendance copy and the community history — contact us at the address below and we will remove it. Attendo has no in-app account deletion, and signing out does not delete anything.

Third-party services

The optional online features rely on:

  • Supabase — backend hosting for the community board and for the private copy of your attendance. Protected by row-level rules that scope each account's data to that account.
  • GitHub — authentication when you choose to sign in, and the source of app update releases, which the app checks for.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — the anonymous aggregate statistics described above.

Each is governed by its own privacy policy.

Children

Attendo is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.
