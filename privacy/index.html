Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026 · Applies to the Attendo Android app and this website.

The short version

Attendo is local-first. Your attendance data, timetable, and settings live on your phone. Nothing about your attendance is sent anywhere unless you deliberately use an optional online feature. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't build advertising profiles.

What stays on your device

  • Your courses, timetable, attendance records, notes, and settings — stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Backups and exports — complete app backups are saved in JSON format, while attendance data can be exported in CSV format. Files are created only when you choose to export and are not uploaded by the app.

Optional community reporting

Not in a published release. Community reporting is under development and is not part of the published v1.1 release. This section describes what it will involve once it ships.

Attendo can send anonymous room-availability reports to a shared community board so other students can see which rooms are free right now. If you use this feature, your report content (room, time, observation type) and a randomly generated community identifier are sent to our backend and shown to other users. Community features can be used without an account.

GitHub sign-in

Not in a published release. GitHub sign-in is under development and is not part of the published v1.1 release. This section describes what it will involve once it ships.

Attendo offers optional sign-in with GitHub. If you use it, GitHub shares your name, email address, and profile picture with Attendo. We use this information only to create and recognise your account and to keep your community history attached to it. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it with anyone else. You can disconnect or delete your account at any time (see below).

Analytics

The app collects anonymous, aggregate usage statistics that help us understand whether and how widely features are used — for example, that a screen was opened a certain number of times. These statistics are not linked to your name, email, or the content of your attendance records.

What we never do

  • Sell your personal data.
  • Share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Read or upload your attendance data, backups, or exports.
  • Send you marketing or sell advertising.

Data retention and deletion

  • Device data: delete it any time by clearing Attendo's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Community data: signing out of the community feature removes the community identity from your device; reports you have contributed remain on the community board unless you ask us to remove them.
  • Account data: to delete your account and its community history, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Third-party services

The optional online features rely on Supabase (backend hosting) and, if you sign in, GitHub (authentication); neither is part of the published v1.1 release. Each is governed by its own privacy policy. Updates for the app are checked against GitHub Releases.

Children

Attendo is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the change will be noted here with a new effective date.
