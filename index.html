Attendo

Attendance tracking and room lookup for students of the Faculty of Technology, University of Delhi.

Keep your attendance — honestly

Attendo records whether you were in each class your timetable says you had, counted in hours — so leaving a two-hour lab halfway through counts as exactly half, not a coin flip. Every course gets its own percentage and target, and the app tells you how many classes you can still afford to miss, or how many you must attend to get back on track.

  • A normal day in one tap; a missed day in one action
  • Cancelled, shortened, moved, and extra classes — with notes
  • A colour-coded semester calendar, per course and overall
  • Semester dates, holidays, working Saturdays, mid-semester joins
  • Backup and restore to a single file you control, and attendance export as CSV at any time
  • A PDF semester record when a new semester begins

Find rooms

Which rooms are free right now, and what any room's whole week looks like. The faculty's printed timetable ships inside the app.

Your data stays on your phone

Attendo is local-first. Everything you record stays on your device until you export it yourself. Optional community features and GitHub sign-in are under development and are not part of the published v1.1 release; the privacy policy describes what they will involve.

Get the app

Attendo is free and open source (MIT), and is not on any app store.
