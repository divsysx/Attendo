Attendo

Attendance tracking and room lookup for students of the Faculty of Technology, University of Delhi.

Keep your attendance — honestly

Attendo records whether you were in each class your timetable says you had, counted in hours — so leaving a two-hour lab halfway through counts as exactly half, not a coin flip. Every course gets its own percentage and target, and the app tells you how many classes you can still afford to miss, or how many you must attend to get back on track.

  • A normal day in one tap; a missed day in one action
  • Cancelled, shortened, moved, and extra classes — with notes
  • A colour-coded semester calendar, per course and overall
  • An overall target, a default for new courses, and a bulk apply — on their own target screen, with each course's own target kept separate
  • Semester dates, holidays and working Saturdays, each on its own screen
  • Backup and restore to a single file you control, and attendance export as CSV at any time
  • A PDF semester record when a new semester begins

Find rooms

Which rooms are free right now, and what any room's whole week looks like. The faculty's printed timetable ships inside the app, covering 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday — and when the faculty reissues it mid-semester, the app moves your courses onto the new hours without disturbing anything you have already recorded.

See what is happening now

The timetable is the plan; the Community feature is what is actually going on. From any room's screen, report what you find — a room the timetable calls free that is occupied, a busy one that is free, a class that moved — and ask the people in it a quick poll. Reports are filed under a random anonymous ID: no name, no email, nothing that identifies you, and the feature works with no account at all.

Keep two phones in step

Sign in with GitHub and Attendo keeps a private copy of your attendance for your account — courses and their targets, timetable, class records, semesters, and the settings that go with them. A second phone, or the same phone after a reinstall, carries on where you left off, and syncing runs on its own whenever you are online; Sync now is there when you want it immediately.

It is entirely optional, and it is the only thing about your attendance that ever leaves your device. Without it the app is purely local. Attendo never sees your GitHub password — sign-in happens on GitHub's own page — and it reads nothing from your GitHub profile beyond which account you are.

Your data is yours

Attendo is local-first. What you record stays on your phone until you export it yourself or deliberately sign in, as above. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't build advertising profiles — see the privacy policy for exactly what is stored, what is sent, and what is not.
